A HUG FOR YOU - COMPLETE WEBSITE

1. Extract this ZIP first.
2. Keep index.html, style.css, and script.js in the same folder.
3. Open index.html in your browser.

No installation or internet connection is needed.

EDITING
index.html: messages, signature, and illustrations.
style.css: colors, layout, and animations.
script.js: page navigation and the hug animation.

To change the sender name, replace Adil in index.html.

The last page shows two teddy bears labeled me and you hugging.
The page does not save answers or track visitors.
